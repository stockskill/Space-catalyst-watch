## Source hierarchy

1. SEC/EDGAR, company filings, earnings releases, official investor presentations
2. NASA, DoD, FCC and official procurement or regulatory documents
3. Company operational releases and named customer/carrier releases
4. Reuters or comparable reporting for context and attributed reaction
5. Social media only as an unverified lead; never as proof

Record the original publication date, the actual event date when different, and the retrieval time for a market quote.
