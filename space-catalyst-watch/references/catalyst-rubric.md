# Catalyst rubric

## Decide whether an update is material

Call an item material only if it plausibly changes at least one of these:

- revenue, margin, backlog, cash runway, dilution risk, or funding cost;
- launch, deployment, regulatory, carrier, or mission timeline;
- addressable market, customer access, or contract visibility;
- probability of a stated operational milestone.

Routine marketing updates, unsourced rumours, generic conference appearances, and old news recycled as new information are not material.

## Event-to-signal test

Before assigning BUY or SELL, answer all four questions:

1. What was the prior stated expectation?
2. What newly verified fact differs from it?
3. Why can that difference matter within the chosen horizon?
4. What filing, result, milestone, or financing disclosure would disprove the interpretation?

If any answer is missing, use NEUTRAL / WATCH and state what evidence is missing.

## Mandatory risk pass

Check the latest available filing or earnings material for:

- cash and restricted cash; operating cash flow; debt and maturities;
- ATM programs, shelf registrations, convertibles, warrants, share counts, and recent issuance;
- revised guidance, margin pressure, contract loss provisions, and customer concentration;
- delayed launches, deployment or certification dependencies; and
- unusual liquidity or a stale/closed-market price quote.

Do not convert a price move into a causal claim unless a reliable source attributes it. Label causal interpretation as inference.

## Minimum multi-company summary

| Ticker | New verified event | Expectation change | Research status | Next decisive date/event |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `No material update` rather than inventing a reason to discuss a ticker.
